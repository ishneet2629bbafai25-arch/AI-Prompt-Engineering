AI Prompt Engineering & Business Problem-Solving
📌 Overview

This repository contains a structured collection of AI prompts and their results, designed to demonstrate how simple prompts can be improved into more detailed, reliable, and business-focused prompts.

The project covers different areas such as Marketing, Finance, Operations, Human Resources, Customer Service, Business Problem-Solving, Research, and AI-assisted decision-making.

The prompts are organized into different levels:

Version 1 – Basic Prompts: Simple and general questions.
Version 2 – Refined Prompts: Prompts with clearer requirements, constraints, and output formats.
Version 3 – Most Refined Prompts: Detailed prompts containing role, context, task, constraints, format, assumptions, and verification requirements.
🎯 Objectives
Understand the fundamentals of effective prompt writing.
Compare basic prompts with refined prompts.
Improve the quality and usefulness of AI-generated responses.
Apply AI to practical business problems.
Learn how to provide clear context, constraints, and expected output formats.
Encourage verification of AI-generated facts, statistics, and assumptions.
Separate facts, interpretations, assumptions, and recommendations.

🧠 Prompt Engineering Approach

The project demonstrates how prompt quality improves when additional information is provided.

A refined prompt can include:

Role → Context → Task → Constraints → Output Format → Assumptions → Verification

For example, instead of asking:

"Create a marketing campaign."

A more refined prompt can specify the target audience, budget, marketing channels, campaign duration, expected benefits, and required output format.

🔍 Responsible AI & Verification

An important part of this project is recognizing that AI-generated information should not automatically be treated as factual.

The research prompts emphasize:

Checking original sources
Verifying important claims and statistics
Checking publication dates and context
Identifying unsupported assumptions
Avoiding invented sources or data
Separating evidence from interpretation and recommendations
Conducting final human review
